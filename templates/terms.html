1. Operational Alignment with MAITRI Portal

AnumatiSetu is envisioned as an intelligent front-end companion and pre-check orchestration assistant that functions alongside Maharashtra’s official MAITRI (Maharashtra Industry, Trade and Investment Facilitation Cell) single-window portal.

  • No Legal Clearance Issuance: Final statutory approvals, No-Objection Certificates (NOCs), consent letters, and formal rejections remain the sovereign prerogative of the concerned Government Authorities.
  • Complementary Pre-Checks: Our OCR and validation logic is intended to help entrepreneurs verify dossier completeness before formal government submission, reducing back-and-forth scrutiny queries.
