About AnumatiSetu

Intelligent Industrial Approval Assistance and Orchestration Layer for the Government of Maharashtra.

Core Government Positioning Statement

AnumatiSetu does NOT replace MAITRI or MAITRI 2.0. It is an intelligent assistance, document pre-validation, and workflow orchestration layer designed to work synergistically with Maharashtra’s existing single-window ecosystem. Final approval, rejection, regulatory scrutiny, and statutory decisions always remain strictly with the concerned Government Department or Competent Authority.

Core Platform Features

Smart Approval Discovery

Maps 128+ industrial services based on enterprise scale, Red/Orange/Green classification, and location.

Document Pre-check & OCR Validation

Identifies missing documents (like ETP schematics) before submission, preventing rejection loops.

RTS SLA Deadline Tracking

Visual progress meters with statutory days remaining under Maharashtra Right to Services Act.

Joint Inspection Scheduling

Single-window field inspection calendar with assigned divisional inspectors.
