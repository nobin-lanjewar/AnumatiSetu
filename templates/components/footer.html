ANUMATISETU

Intelligent Industrial Approval Assistance & Orchestration Platform. Empowering entrepreneurs across Maharashtra with automated pre-checks, SLA visibility, and unified departmental tracking.

Important Government Positioning: AnumatiSetu works synergistically alongside MAITRI / MAITRI 2.0. Final approval, rejection, or regulatory decision always remains with the concerned Government Department / Authority.

  • Document Pre-check Engine
  • Unified SLA Tracking

  • Compliance Shield & Renewals
  • Incentive Schemes (PSI 2019)

  • Industrial Safety & Health (DISH)
  • Pollution Control Board (MPCB)
  • MIDC Fire Services
