The Approval Journey

How AnumatiSetu Works

A transparent, step-by-step assistance and orchestration pathway connecting Maharashtra entrepreneurs with reviewing authorities.

Figure 2.0 — Orchestrated 5-Stage Clearance Architecture Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26130)
01

1. Business Registration & Profile Setup

Stage 1

The entrepreneur registers their enterprise profile (e.g. manufacturing sector, district location, investment scale, and projected workforce). AnumatiSetu analyzes the industrial scale, sector categorization (Red/Orange/Green), and MIDC plot location to instantly establish the regulatory baseline.

02

2. Prepare Documents & Intelligent Pre-check

Stage 2

Upload blueprints, land lease deeds, PAN, and technical schematics. AnumatiSetu’s pre-validation engine performs OCR metadata extraction, size and format verification, and cross-references mandatory annexures. Any missing documents (such as ETP specifications) are flagged before submission, eliminating initial departmental rejection loops.

03

3. Concurrent Department Processing & Scrutiny

Stage 3

Instead of sequential handoffs, applications are concurrently forwarded to relevant statutory departments — Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health (DISH), Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB), MIDC Fire Services, and MSEDCL. Real-time RTS SLA countdown timers track progress transparently.

04

4. Coordinated Field Inspection

Stage 4

Where on-site verification is statutorily mandated (such as factory layout safety audits or fire hydrant simulations), inspections are scheduled through a unified calendar with assigned divisional officers. Both entrepreneur and inspector have real-time visibility into the scheduled time slot and site expectations.

05

5. Final Government Decision & Ongoing Compliance

Stage 5

The competent reviewing officer (e.g. Chief Inspector of Factories or Sub-Regional Officer) issues the digital approval certificate. AnumatiSetu automatically provisions an ongoing Compliance Shield with automated alerts for periodic renewal dates (Form 4, CTO renewal, Form B fire drill).
