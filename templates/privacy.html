Privacy Policy & Industrial Data Security

How AnumatiSetu safeguards entrepreneur identities, factory blueprints, financial filings, and statutory application data.

Last Updated: October 2026 • SIH26130 Smart India Hackathon Prototype Security Baseline
Smart India Hackathon 2026 Academic Sandbox Notice: AnumatiSetu is an innovative technical prototype developed for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement SIH26130). It is not an official Government of Maharashtra website. Real-world statutory submissions must be conducted through the official MAITRI single-window portal (maitri.mahaonline.gov.in).

1. Purpose & Scope of Data Collection

AnumatiSetu operates as an intelligent orchestration layer designed to assist industrial enterprises in Maharashtra with clearance discovery, automated document pre-validation, and multi-departmental tracking.

To evaluate approval eligibility and simulate regulatory workflows, the platform processes:

  • Enterprise Identity: Company name, Udyam Registration number, PAN, GSTIN, and registered factory location.
  • Technical Documentation: Factory layout blueprints, plant machinery schematics, Environmental Management Plans (EMP), and fire egress drawings.
  • Operational Metrics: Connected electricity load (KVA), water consumption (KLD), boiler capacities, workforce numbers, and projected capital investment.
  • Account Contact Information: Authorized representative name, official email address, and mobile phone number for status dispatches.

2. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

In strict adherence to the principles of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

Purpose Limitation

Data uploaded is utilized strictly for approval eligibility computation and mock statutory review simulation.

Zero Commercial Monetization

No enterprise documents or applicant personal records are sold, traded, or shared with commercial advertisers.

Consent-Driven Uploads

Every document upload requires explicit user consent, clearly indicating which department's pre-check criteria it fulfills.

Right to Erasure

Entrepreneurs can request complete purging of mock uploaded blueprints, test attachments, and test profiles at any time.

3. Storage & Cryptographic Standards

All digital assets, including simulated factory blueprints, inspection photos, and identity proofs, are protected using industry-standard protocols:

  • In-Transit Security: Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with forward secrecy on all endpoints.
  • At-Rest Encryption: Database records and media repositories utilize AES-256 bit encryption.
  • Access Control: Role-based access control (RBAC) isolates applicant documents so that only the applicant and assigned departmental scrutiny officers have inspection privileges.

4. Sandbox Data Hygiene & Disposal

Because this deployment serves as a demonstration prototype for Hackathon evaluations:

  • Sample demonstration records (e.g. Amol Foods Pvt. Ltd., Nashik) utilize fictitious or generalized industrial parameters.
  • Evaluators and testing users are strongly advised not to upload proprietary trade secrets, unpatented designs, or classified government records.
  • Evaluation sandbox databases are periodically flushed and re-seeded to default demonstration baselines.
