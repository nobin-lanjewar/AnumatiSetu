The Problem

Industrial Approvals Shouldn’t Feel Fragmented

Entrepreneurs face a siloed regulatory landscape with uncoordinated timelines, missing documents, and opaque departmental handoffs.

01

Multiple Departments

Entrepreneurs must navigate disparate portals for DISH, MPCB, Fire, and Local Authorities without unified synchronization.

02

Different Requirements

Unclear guidelines on what approvals apply based on investment scale, worker count, or industrial red/orange/green zones.

03

Incomplete Documents

Applications submitted with missing annexures result in formal rejection loops and weeks of administrative delays.

04

Manual Follow-ups

Lack of transparent SLA countdown timers forces business founders into repetitive in-person departmental inquiries.

05

Inspection Coordination

Separate site audits by multiple field officers without a coordinated schedule disrupt factory construction progress.

06

Renewal Deadlines

Missed statutory licence renewal cycles trigger compounding financial penalties and risk factory operational closures.
